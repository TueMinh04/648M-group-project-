# Ethics framework (working notes)
Principle: **AI assists; clinicians remain accountable.**
| Issue | Risk | Safeguard | Remaining limitation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patient consent | Consent is not always obtained when clinical photographs are taken | Strong policy ensuring consent practices inform patients fully and data is strictly for education | Retrospective datasets may lack explicit consent | IRB Guidelines |
| Privacy & de-identification | Identifiable patient info published without proper redaction | According to IRB, any patient info where identifiable must be de-identified prior to publication | Edge-case biometric re-identification | IRB Standards |
| Cybersecurity & AI infrastructure | AI technologies leave healthcare vulnerable to attackers and breaches | End-to-end encryption, MFA, routine patching, corporate risk management, vendor supervision, and cybersecurity training | Evolving attack vectors and zero-days | Healthcare Cybersecurity Standards |
| AI safety & accuracy | Simulated AI feedback reinforces incorrect technique | "Human-in-the-loop" educator review and override capability | Instructor review time bottlenecks | to be added |
| Bias & fairness | Procedure data under-represents diverse demographics | Mandatory diversity audits and multi-institutional data recruitment | Availability of rare clinical recordings | to be added |
| Transparency of feedback | Opaque scoring causing learner distrust | Rule-based, explainable kinematic metrics and baseline comparisons | Metric simplification of complex clinical touch | to be added |
| Learner data privacy | Performance telemetry misused for high-stakes grading | Purpose limitation policies; learner ownership of data | Institutional record retention policies | to be added |
| Accessibility & equity | High-spec VR hardware creates disparity across programs | Scalable, web-accessible modes and low-spec device optimization | Haptic feedback differences | to be added |
| Human oversight | Reliance on automated training without clinician guidance | Virtucare acts as a supplement; faculty retains final sign-off | Faculty scheduling availability | to be added |
