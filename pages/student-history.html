Curated Recommendations
1. Female Foley Catheter: Extra Advancement Depth

In trials #02 and #13, balloon inflation commenced immediately upon first urine droplet observation. In female anatomy, always advance an additional 2.5–3 cm to guarantee the balloon is fully intravesical prior to inflating 10mL sterile water.

2. Peripheral IV: Tourniquet Release Timing

Remember to unclip the venous tourniquet prior to connection and flush of the saline extension set. Releasing tension earlier prevents vein rupture and hematoma formation in fragile vessels.

3. Nasogastric Tube: Swallow Cue Synchronization

Maintain rhythm during posterior oropharyngeal pass by advancing 5–7 cm increments exactly concurrent with simulated patient swallows to avert tracheal deflection.
