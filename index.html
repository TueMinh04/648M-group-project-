1. The Problem

Current clinical training relies heavily on physical simulation labs or direct patient interaction. These existing methods face several critical shortcomings:

Shortcomings of Existing Technology

  • Limited Accessibility: Physical simulation labs are expensive and require scheduled access.

  • High Resource Demands: Require constant human supervision and expert availability for feedback.

  • Lack of Personalization: Standardized mannequins offer uniform scenarios that don't adapt to individual learner needs.

The Need for Change

There is a growing need for a scalable, low-stakes environment where learners can practice procedures with real-time, personalized feedback without risking patient safety or overwhelming limited clinical resources.

2. Our Proposal: VIRTUCARE

We propose VIRTUCARE, an AI-assisted clinical simulation platform that addresses the shortcomings of existing methods by transforming consented clinical procedure recordings into interactive, personalized, and scalable training scenarios.

1. Capture & Protect

Consented recordings are collected, and identifiers are detected and removed for privacy.

2. Understand & Transform

AI analyzes the structure of the procedure to generate dynamic interactive scenarios.

3. Validate & Practice

Clinicians review the content. Learners practice with AI oversight.

4. Social Implications

How the tool impacts society and our strategies to address these impacts.

Accessibility & Equity

Impact: High-end VR devices may exclude underfunded institutions.

Solution: Provide a browser-based, lightweight 2D alternative alongside the VR mode.

Bias in Training Data

Impact: Source recordings may under-represent certain demographics or rare conditions.

Solution: Conduct mandatory audits of source diversity and actively recruit varied clinical scenarios.

5. Ethical Considerations

Addressing the moral challenges of using AI in clinical education.

Consent

Patients may not fully understand how their recordings are used. We enforce layered, easily withdrawable consent.

Safety & Accuracy

AI feedback might be incorrect. We enforce a "Human-in-the-loop" policy where educators can override AI.

Privacy

Learner performance data must be protected. We guarantee clear purpose limits and give learners control over their data.
