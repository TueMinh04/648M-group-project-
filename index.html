1. Topic Selection & Overview

Our project focuses on AI in Procedural Training. We are proposing VIRTUCARE, an AI-assisted clinical simulation concept.

Anticipated Benefits

  • Provides a safe, low-stakes environment for clinical practice.

  • Scalable training that doesn't rely on constant human supervision.

  • Personalized feedback powered by AI algorithms.

Potential Shortcomings

  • Risk of AI hallucinations or incorrect clinical feedback.

  • High development costs and required computational resources.

  • Lack of tactile feedback compared to real-world practice.

2. Proposal: How It Works

VIRTUCARE leverages consented clinical procedure recordings and transforms them into interactive scenarios. AI analyzes the procedure structure, and students can practice virtually while receiving real-time AI-assisted feedback.

1. Capture & Protect

Consented recordings are collected, and identifiers are detected and removed for privacy.

2. Understand & Transform

AI analyzes the structure of the procedure to generate dynamic interactive scenarios.

3. Validate & Practice

Clinicians review the content. Learners practice with AI oversight.

3. Social Implications

How the tool impacts society and our strategies to address these impacts.

Accessibility & Equity

Impact: High-end VR devices may exclude underfunded institutions.

Solution: Provide a browser-based, lightweight 2D alternative alongside the VR mode.

Bias in Training Data

Impact: Source recordings may under-represent certain demographics or rare conditions.

Solution: Conduct mandatory audits of source diversity and actively recruit varied clinical scenarios.

4. Ethical Considerations

Addressing the moral challenges of using AI in clinical education.

Consent

Patients may not fully understand how their recordings are used. We enforce layered, easily withdrawable consent.

Safety & Accuracy

AI feedback might be incorrect. We enforce a "Human-in-the-loop" policy where educators can override AI.

Privacy

Learner performance data must be protected. We guarantee clear purpose limits and give learners control over their data.
