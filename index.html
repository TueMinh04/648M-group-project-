Stage 01 · Ethical Governance & Patient Autonomy
IRB Safeguard Consent Gate

Patient Consent Verification & Privacy De-Identification

In healthcare informatics, clinical video must never become an unconsented training commodity. Virtucare enforces an explicit ethical consent verification gate before any spatial AI processing is permitted:

1. Tiered Informed Consent
Verifies explicit dynamic consent authorizing clinical video strictly for educational simulation, separating care from AI training (TCPS 2 Art. 3.1).
2. Acute & SDM Protocols
Governs emergent bedside intubations via Substitute Decision-Maker delayed consent, with synthetic anatomical fallback if consent is unobtainable.
3. Algorithmic De-Identification
Automated neural redaction purges 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor identifiers: facial geometry, room clocks, voice audio, and identifying marks.
4. Dynamic Right of Erasure
Guarantees the patient's "Right to be Forgotten" via automated cryptographic purging of raw video and 3D Gaussian weights within 24 hours.
